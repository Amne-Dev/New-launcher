# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

The launcher serves casual players who want to play a selected installation immediately and modpack-heavy players who need dependable access to their installations, mods, and modpacks. Both groups use a desktop launcher where a fast, obvious launch action is the primary workflow.

## Product Purpose

New Launcher is a lightweight, cross-platform Minecraft launcher for Windows and Linux. It makes launching the selected installation immediate while keeping deeper mod and modpack management available without slowing down the everyday experience. Success means players can trust it as a fast personal launcher that respects their privacy and supports offline skin customization.

## Positioning

A lightweight, fully customizable and privacy-focused Minecraft launcher that lets offline players use their own skin.

## Brand Personality

Fast, playful, and powerful. The launcher should feel like a real desktop application: responsive, direct, and personal rather than like a browser tab packaged as an app.

## Anti-references

Avoid an Electron or generic web-app feel: browser-like chrome, delayed interaction feedback, heavy resource use, and interface motion that makes simple tasks feel slower. Evolve the current launcher’s dark custom identity rather than replacing it with a generic platform-native theme.

## Design Principles

1. Put Play first: the selected installation and its launch action are always immediately legible.
2. Make power optional: modpack and customization tools are close at hand but never obstruct a quick launch.
3. Feel native in motion: window state changes, scrolling, lists, and transitions respond directly and predictably.
4. Treat privacy as a product promise: offline play and personal skins are first-class, understandable features.
5. Preserve personal control: customization is rich but organized, with no hidden cost to performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide keyboard navigation, readable contrast, scalable text, and reduced-motion support. No additional accessibility requirements were specified.
